What Are Fixed Indexed Annuity (FIA) Leads?

By Gregory Stevenson, producing FIA agent & author of Index Annuity Secrets · Updated October 2026

A fixed indexed annuity (FIA) lead is the contact information of a real person who has expressed interest in a fixed indexed annuity — typically someone near or in retirement who wants growth potential tied to a market index with protection from market losses. For a licensed agent, an FIA lead is a prospect to call, educate, and potentially write an annuity case for.

In one sentence: an FIA lead is a retirement-minded consumer who raised their hand for indexed-annuity information — and that you, a licensed agent, can follow up with.

How are FIA leads generated?

Good FIA leads come from opt-in marketing: ads, educational content, quizzes, or free resources (like a book on how indexed annuities work) that invite people interested in protecting their retirement savings to request more information. When they submit their details, they become a lead. The best programs then introduce that prospect to a specific agent and keep them warm with follow-up until the agent connects.

Lower-quality "leads" are scraped lists, cold data, or aged leads resold many times — people who never actually asked to hear from an annuity agent. Those convert poorly and can create compliance headaches.

Exclusive vs. shared FIA leads

FIA leads are sold two ways. Shared leads go to several agents at once, so you're racing others to the phone. Exclusive leads go to one agent only. Exclusivity usually costs more per lead but converts far better, because the prospect isn't being called by five people. (We go deep on this in Exclusive vs. Shared Annuity Leads.)

What do FIA leads cost?

Pricing varies widely by exclusivity and quality. Shared leads can be a few dollars each; exclusive, nurtured leads cost more but close better. Who buys FIA leads?

Licensed life and annuity agents and agency owners — from solo producers who want a steadier flow of appointments to teams scaling their annuity production. To work FIA leads you must hold the proper state licenses and follow all applicable contact and suitability rules.

What makes an FIA lead actually close?

  • Exclusivity — you're the only agent calling.
  • A warm introduction — the prospect knows your name and expects you.
  • Ongoing nurture — education and follow-up that keep them engaged until you reach them.
  • Speed and consistency — fast first contact, then steady follow-up over weeks.
