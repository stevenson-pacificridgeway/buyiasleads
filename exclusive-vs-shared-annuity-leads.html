Exclusive vs. Shared Annuity Leads: Which Actually Make Agents More Money?

By Gregory Stevenson, producing FIA agent & author of Index Annuity Secrets · Updated October 2026

If you've shopped for annuity leads, you've seen the split: cheap shared leads sold to several agents at once, and pricier exclusive leads sold to one agent only. The sticker price makes shared leads look like the smart buy. In practice, the exclusive lead is almost always cheaper per sale — and sale cost is the only number that matters.

What's the actual difference?

A shared lead is sold to 3–8 agents. You're racing everyone else to the phone, the prospect gets called repeatedly, and by the time you connect they're annoyed or already talking to someone. An exclusive lead is generated for one agent. No race, no lead fatigue, and — done right — the prospect is expecting your call because they were introduced to you by name.

The numbers that decide it

Three metrics separate a good lead program from a money pit: contact rate (how many you actually reach), close rate (how many turn into cases), and true cost per sale. Here's the pattern most agents see:

 Shared leadsExclusive leads
Agents calling the same person3–81 (you)
Contact rateLower (lead fatigue)Higher (warm, expecting you)
Close rateLowerHigher
Price per leadLowerHigher
Cost per closed caseUsually higherUsually lower

A $15 shared lead feels great until you've bought 40 of them, reached a handful, and closed none. A $20 exclusive lead that you can actually reach — and who already knows your name — wins on the only scoreboard that counts: closed annuity cases per dollar spent.

"Exclusive" isn't enough on its own

Exclusivity solves the race. It doesn't solve the cold call. The best programs pair exclusivity with nurture: the prospect is introduced to you the moment they opt in, then kept warm with education and follow-up until you reach them. At BuyIASLeads that means a 52-week email and text campaign, an annuity education hub, an AI assistant that answers prospect questions, and a free book — all working under your name between your calls.

When shared leads can make sense

If you have a dialer, a thick skin, and the time to hammer high volume at low cost, shared leads can work as a numbers game. For most agents who'd rather have fewer, warmer conversations and protect their time, exclusive-and-nurtured wins.

Bottom line

Don't shop on price per lead. Shop on cost per closed case. Exclusive, nurtured annuity leads usually cost more up front and less per sale — and one indexed-annuity case typically pays for a whole package several times over.
