Annuity Leads for Insurance Agents: The Complete Guide

By Gregory Stevenson, producing FIA agent & author of Index Annuity Secrets · Updated October 2026
In short: annuity leads are retirement-minded consumers who raised their hand for annuity information. For insurance agents, the ones worth buying are exclusive, generated from real opt-ins, introduced under your name, and nurtured — because the money is made by reaching and sitting with prospects, not by buying the most leads for the least money.

If you sell life and annuity products, a steady flow of good annuity prospects is the difference between a feast-or-famine pipeline and a predictable one. This guide pulls together everything an insurance agent needs to know about annuity leads — and links to deeper breakdowns on each piece.

What annuity leads are

An annuity lead is the contact information of a real person interested in an annuity — typically someone near or in retirement who wants growth potential with protection from market losses. Most agents focus on fixed indexed annuity (FIA) leads. (Full explainer: What Are FIA Leads?)

Exclusive vs. shared — the choice that matters most

Shared leads go to several agents at once; exclusive leads go to you alone. Exclusivity costs more but drives higher contact and close rates, because the prospect isn't being called by five people. For most agents, exclusive is the only option worth paying for. (Deep dive: Exclusive vs. Shared.)

What annuity leads cost

Shared leads can be a few dollars each; exclusive FIA leads typically run $15–$50+ each. Price is driven by exclusivity, how the lead was generated, and whether it comes with nurture and a CRM. (Full breakdown: How Much Do Annuity Leads Cost?)

How to buy them without wasting money

Confirm you're licensed, choose exclusive over shared, vet the provider (how are leads generated, is there nurture, who runs it, what's the replacement policy), start with a right-sized package, and measure your numbers before scaling. (Step-by-step: How to Buy Annuity Leads, and the buyer's checklist.)

How to turn leads into closed cases

Every conversion rate comes down to whether you can reach people. The levers are exclusivity (you're the only one calling), a warm introduction (the prospect knows your name), ongoing nurture (education that keeps them engaged), and speed plus consistency (fast first contact, steady follow-up). Get those right and the commission math does the heavy lifting. (See Annuity Lead ROI.)
